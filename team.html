Jeremy Theisen

Founder

Jeremy is a seasoned restaurant industry executive with more than 20 years of experience driving revenue growth through technology, culture, and strategic innovation.

He served as Chief Growth Officer at Craveworthy Brands, where he led initiatives that significantly increased systemwide revenue. Before that, he was Chief Growth Officer at FAT Brands, overseeing franchise development and leading digital strategies designed to accelerate sales and profitability for franchisees.

Earlier, as Chief Sales Officer at Punchh, Jeremy helped scale the company from launch to more than $50 million in revenue before its acquisition by PAR Technology Corporation.

Jeremy served on the Toast Customer Advisory Board (2023–2024) and was recognized as a 2025 QSR Digital Disruptor for his impact on restaurant technology and growth.

Kim DeCarolis

Founder

Kim is a restaurant industry leader with 14 years of experience across marketing, technology, and operations — including more than a decade driving organizational growth through sales leadership in restaurant operations and technology.

She began her career in PCI compliance and network security at NuArx before moving into restaurant technology at Punchh, where she became the first woman named to the executive team and served as Vice President of Sales — playing a pivotal role in scaling the company to $50 million in revenue prior to its acquisition.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Kim led enterprise sales for reservations, ordering, and CRM platforms at SevenRooms, and later expanded into food safety, training, and operations with PathSpot. She then transitioned to the brand side, serving as Director of Strategic Franchise Sales at FAT Brands and working with Craveworthy and Smalls Sliders to build high-growth franchise partnerships and solve operational challenges.

Kim is a member of the Women's Foodservice Forum and WIRL, where she actively contributes to advancing women in the restaurant industry.
