About

Golf-inspired restaurant consulting.

We blend technology selection, operational excellence, and franchise development through a golf-inspired lens rooted in integrity — helping restaurant brands improve efficiency, grow margins, and scale with confidence.

Our approach

Experience on both sides of the table.

Our approach is shaped by experience on both sides of the table. For more than 35 combined years, we've worked with leading restaurant brands and the technology providers that serve them. We've also been directly involved in growing multi-brand restaurant concepts, giving us a clear view of what actually works once ideas meet operations.

We start with the P&L. By understanding how the business truly performs, we identify where growth is being left on the table and where costs can be reduced without hurting the operation. From there, we help brands choose the right technology and partners to solve real problems, not create new ones.

Because of our deep industry network, we don't just make recommendations. We help negotiate favorable terms, reduce unnecessary spend, and ensure vendors are aligned with the brand's long-term goals. The result is practical guidance, smarter decisions, and solutions that hold up in real restaurants.

  • Independent. No vendor commissions. No referral fees. Ever.
  • Operator-minded. We've sat in the seat. We know what holds up in real operations.
  • Honest. Clear guidance, honest feedback, decisions made in the best interest of your business.
  • Senior. The founders you meet are the founders who run the work.

A note on the name

Like a good golf game, foundation is key.

In golf, every shot is counted, the rules apply even when no one is watching, and results speak for themselves. We bring that same standard to the restaurants we advise — reading the situation, choosing the right shot, and executing with discipline.
