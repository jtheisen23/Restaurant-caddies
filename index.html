What we do

End-to-end advisory for the modern restaurant brand.

We sit on your side of the table — evaluating, negotiating, and implementing the systems and partnerships that drive guest loyalty, operational margin, and franchise growth.

Technology Stack Strategy

Independent evaluation of POS, online ordering, loyalty, CDP, CRM, KDS, and back-office systems — mapped to your concept, growth stage, and unit economics.

  • Current-state audit & gap analysis
  • Vendor short-listing & RFP management
  • Total cost of ownership modeling

Loyalty & Guest Engagement

Design and launch loyalty programs that move the needle — from points-based to subscription, surprise-and-delight, and behavioral tiers — with the data plumbing to prove ROI.

  • Program design & economic modeling
  • Platform selection & implementation
  • Lifecycle & reactivation campaigns

Digital & Third-Party Ordering

Optimize your direct ordering channels and rebalance the third-party marketplace economics — menus, pricing, commissions, sponsored placements, and integrations.

  • Web & app ordering selection
  • Marketplace negotiation (DSPs)
  • Channel-level P&L optimization

CDP & CRM Architecture

Unify guest data across POS, ordering, loyalty, Wi-Fi, and reservations — and turn it into segmented, automated, revenue-driving lifecycle marketing.

  • Data model & identity resolution
  • Platform selection & integration
  • Marketing automation playbooks

AI Strategy & Roadmap

Cut through the AI noise. We help you identify the use cases that actually move EBITDA — from drive-thru voice to forecasting, scheduling, menu optimization, and guest service.

  • Use-case prioritization & ROI modeling
  • Vendor & build-vs-buy evaluation
  • Pilot design & rollout governance

Procurement & Negotiation

We negotiate every line of every contract — software, hardware, payments, marketplaces, agencies — and structure terms that protect you as you scale.

  • Contract benchmarking & redlines
  • Renewal & consolidation strategy
  • Performance SLAs & exit clauses
